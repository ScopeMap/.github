## Building got faster. Deciding what to build didn't.

AI collapsed the cost of building. The thinking that turns a rough idea into something worth building stayed slow, manual, and dependent on a skill not everyone has.

ScopeMap closes that gap. Describe an idea in plain language and ScopeMap turns it into a structured, prioritised scope you own and can hand off to anything: an AI coding tool, or an engineering team. It sits above the build tools and feeds them rather than competing with them. Portable and tool-agnostic by design.

Think of it as an extra product person, on tap. Sophisticated enough for corporate teams, simple enough for everyone else to build products without one.

## Who it's for

**Teams doing more with less.** The product thinking that prevents rework is the first thing that gets squeezed when a team is stretched. ScopeMap gives those hours back.

**Everyone else without access to onsite PMs.** Founders, domain experts, and small teams or businesses who got the power to build from AI but don't have the luxury of a product department. No training, no jargon. Describe the idea, get a plan.

Two different people. The same missing capability.

## How it works

1. **Drop a rough idea.** Paste it or just describe it.
2. **Answer the sharp questions.** ScopeMap asks the clarifying questions a senior PM would ask, the ones that surface the real decisions hiding in your idea.
3. **Refine on the canvas.** Your scope appears as cards sorted into Must, Should, Could, and Won't, with user stories, what is out of scope, and what still needs deciding. Move cards, edit them, refine with AI, share for review. Change your mind without starting over.
4. **Hand off and build.** Export, or connect your agent directly. Claude, Cursor, an engineer, a team, or yourself takes it from there.

## Hand it to whatever builds

- **AI coding tools.** Export a `CLAUDE.md` or `AGENTS.md`, or connect Claude Code, Cursor, or Windsurf over MCP and let the agent pull the live scope itself.
- **Your team.** A full PRD as Word, HTML, or Markdown, with branded templates for teams that need them.
- **Your tracker.** A Jira-ready CSV with the hierarchy already mapped.
- **Anything else.** Plain JSON.

## Why it's different

Everything else either locks you in or was never built for this. The prompt-to-app builders keep your plan trapped inside their own pipeline. The classic PM tools were built for roadmaps and tickets, not for handing a scope to an AI coding tool. ScopeMap gives you a structured scope you actually own and can take anywhere.
